// About FIH

FIH is driving the future of smart mobility with integrated E/EA solutions spanning centralized computing, connectivity, and autonomous driving. Our HPC, ZCU, TCU, and ADAS domain controllers enable scalable E/E architectures, OTA updates, 4G/5G and V2X connectivity, secure cloud integration, and intelligent perception and control.

A subsidiary of Hon Hai Technology Group (Foxconn), FIH Mobile Limited (2038.HK) combines global R&D, engineering, and manufacturing capabilities with deep expertise in hardware-software integration. Through its worldwide production network, FIH delivers scalable solutions across automotive electronics, smart manufacturing, and manufacturing equipment and robotics for leading global customers.
